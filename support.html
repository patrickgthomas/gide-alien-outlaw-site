A little help with your landing

Alien Outlaw support

Email support@gidehub.com. Tell us your device model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. A screenshot of the game can help.

How do I play?

Place one saucer in each row, column, and colored region. Saucers cannot touch, including diagonally. Use Mark to cross out squares you have ruled out. The ? button includes How to play.

I’m stuck

Tap Hint for an explanation of the next step. Tap it again to apply the move. Undo lets you back up and try another idea.

Where is the daily puzzle?

Use the daily challenge on the home screen or choose it from New puzzle.

Why can’t I choose a larger board?

Larger boards unlock through wins. The game starts at 5×5 and grows to 12×12. Previously unlocked sizes remain available.

Can I move my progress to another device?

The game stores progress on your device and has no account-based cloud sync. Device backups may include app data depending on your settings. Contact support before deleting the app if you are trying to preserve progress.

Sounds or animations feel distracting

You can turn them off in Settings. There is no countdown, so take your time.
