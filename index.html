Is it available yet?

Alien Outlaw: Crop Circles is being prepared for its first App Store release. The download link will appear here when it is available.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Play offline without signing in. Your game, rewards, and settings are saved on your device.

Can I play at my own pace?

Yes. There is no countdown or time limit. Sounds and animations are optional.

How do bigger boards unlock?

Completing puzzles unlocks larger boards, from 5×5 up to 12×12. You can return to any size you have unlocked.
