Privacy

Alien Outlaw: Crop Circles is an offline, single-player game by Gide. It does not require an account and does not include ads, tracking, or analytics services.

Your game stays on your device

Puzzles, progress, rewards, statistics, and settings are saved locally. The game does not send this information to Gide. Device backup services may include local app data according to your settings. Removing the app may remove its local progress.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store distribution, purchases, and the optional native rating prompt. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device settings and its own privacy policy. These services are separate from the game’s local progress storage.

If you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or private documents.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting services may process technical information needed to deliver pages, such as an IP address and request details. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. This site does not add advertising or analytics scripts.

Your choices

You can change sounds and animations in the game’s settings. Apple purchases, reviews, diagnostics, and device backups are controlled through Apple and your device settings.
